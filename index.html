About

Four years turning designs and requirements into stores that sell and apps that work.

I started with a web development internship in 2019 and have led the Shopify team at Gigalabs since 2022. My day-to-day is scoping work with clients, building it, and keeping it running after launch.

On the Shopify side that means custom Liquid themes, headless Hydrogen storefronts and apps backed by Node.js. Beyond Shopify I build full-stack products: a React/Node/PostgreSQL research platform for oil & gas analysts and a Laravel platform for a business brokerage. I care about clean, maintainable code and clear communication from the first call to the last deploy.
